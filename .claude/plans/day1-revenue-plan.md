# Day-1 revenue plan

Real revenue on day 1 requires selling before/while building, not build-and-hope.

## Buyer: SMB / startup only — not freelancers

Small and medium businesses and startups with an actual invoicing volume/contract relationship
with their clients — not solo freelancers. Pricing should track usage/outcome/activation (per
invoice sent, per client activated, per contract term) rather than a flat per-seat subscription —
see Pricing in `../prd/invoicing-wedge-prd.md`.

## Outreach channels (no Reddit — platform-ban risk on that channel specifically; alternatives
below carry much lower ban/spam risk for this buyer)

1. **Accountant/bookkeeper partners** — the highest-leverage channel for SMB reach: one bookkeeping
   firm serves 20-100 SMB clients. Warm B2B intro, not cold outreach to end users.
2. **LinkedIn** — genuine engagement on posts from SMB owners/operators/accountants about
   invoicing pain, then DM. Slower than Reddit but near-zero ban risk when it's real engagement,
   not templated blasts.
3. **Cold email to SMB owners** (personalized, low-volume, real reply-to/opt-out — CAN-SPAM/GDPR
   compliant) — sourced from public business directories or LinkedIn Sales Navigator, not scraped
   lists.
4. **Comparison/intent content** — "Xero/Wave/Square alternatives" pages on G2/Capterra (vendor
   listing, disclosed), plus a couple of short comparison videos (YouTube Shorts) showing the exact
   failure mode next to our fix. SEO/intent-driven, not platform-outreach-driven, so no ban surface.
5. **Twitter/X build-in-public** — progress posts, not launch announcements; builds an audience
   before we need one and doubles as social proof for cold email/LinkedIn outreach.
6. **Warm network** — direct asks for intros to any SMB owner/founder in your own network first;
   cheapest and highest-trust signal, do this in parallel with the above from day one.

## Mechanism

1. Build the pilot offer: **$10 one-time fee** for early access (not a subscription commitment) —
   lower friction to close during validation; final product pricing is usage/outcome-based per
   contract, decided after Phase 0 (see `../plans/mvp-build-plan.md`).
2. Run channels 1-6 above in parallel for two weeks; track per-channel conversion in
   `../STATE.md` so we know which channel actually produces paying signups, not just interest.
3. Assumption this depends on: SMB/startup buyers convert on channels that don't require a platform
   relationship (Reddit karma, ad account) we don't have yet. Re-evaluate CAC per channel before
   investing further in any one of them.

## Adversarial finding (carried forward, don't re-litigate without new evidence)

Incumbents can patch deliverability cheaper than we can win a switch, and existing users have real
switching costs a point solution doesn't remove. Import (creation mode #3 in the PRD) is our answer
to that, not "avoid existing users" — SMB/startup buyers already on Wave/Xero/Square are back in
scope as long as import actually removes their switching cost in practice, not just in theory.
